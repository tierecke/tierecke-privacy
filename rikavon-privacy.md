# Rikavon Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

The short version: we keep only what the game needs, and we never sell it.

Rikavon is a free card game for Android. This page explains what information the game keeps, why, and what you can ask us to do about it.

## What we keep

- Your player name, country, avatar and gender setting
- Your player number
- Your games, points, friends and achievements
- Any feedback you send us
- On Android, a link to your Google Play Games account, so you can get your player back after reinstalling
- Your IP address, logged together with game and friend activity (for example playing a game, adding a friend or sending a challenge). We use it to fix problems and keep the game fair.

You do not need to create an account or give an email address. The game signs you in anonymously.

All of this is stored on our server.

## What we use it for

Only to run the game: saving your progress, leaderboards, friends, online matches, fixing problems and keeping the game fair. We do not do anything else with it. We do not show ads and we do not build advertising profiles.

## What we never do

We will never sell your data or give it to anyone.

The only outside services that handle it are the ones that run the game for us:

- Our database and game server hosting
- Google Play Games, on Android

## Children

Rikavon is intended for people aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and we will delete the data.

## Your choices and deleting your data

You can ask us at any time to see or delete the data we keep about you, including your player record and the activity log entries tied to it. Email us at the address below with your player name and player number (shown in your profile) and we will take care of it.

## Delete your account and data

To delete your Rikavon player and the data tied to it:

1. Send an email to tierecke@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Rikavon data".
2. Include your player name and player number (shown in your profile in the app).

What we delete: your player profile (name, country, avatar, gender setting), your games, points, friends, achievements, feedback, and the link to your Google Play Games account.

What we keep: nothing that identifies you. Anonymous, aggregate game results may remain.

We complete deletion within 30 days of your request.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
