# Privacy Policy

**Swiss Railway Clock Watch Face** (`net.tierecke.cloque.wear`)

Last updated: 18 October 2026

## The short version

This watch face collects nothing, stores nothing about you, and never
connects to the internet. It requests no permissions at all.

## What it is

The watch face is built in Google's Watch Face Format. It contains **no
executable code**: the installed package is a description of how to draw the
face, plus its images. Wear OS itself does the drawing. There is nothing in
it that could open a network connection, read a file or run in the
background, because there is no code in it to do so.

It declares **no Android permissions**. You can confirm this in Settings,
Apps, on your watch.

## What it displays

To draw the face, Wear OS supplies it with values from your watch. These are
read as they are drawn and are never stored, copied or sent anywhere:

- the current time and date, and whether your watch is set to 12 or 24 hours
- the battery level, for the optional bezel gauge
- your progress towards your daily step goal, for the optional step wedge
- the time of the next sunrise or sunset, for the optional sun mark

The sunrise and sunset time comes from a standard Wear OS complication
provided by your watch, which calculates it from your location. **This watch
face has no location permission and never receives your location.** It
receives only a time such as "18:20" and turns it into a position on the
dial. If your watch has no location fix the complication supplies nothing,
and the sun mark simply does not appear.

## Your settings

Your choices, such as the dial colour or whether the bezel is shown, are
stored by Wear OS on your watch as part of its own watch face settings. They
are not stored by us and are not transmitted.

## Data sharing

None. There are no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no
tracking and no third-party libraries of any kind.

## Children

The watch face collects no data from anyone, including children.
